Publications · Contact · Google Scholar · OSF

I work as an associate professor of sociology at CREST/ENSAE, am a faculty member of the department of economics at Institut Polytechnique de Paris, and I’m an associate member of Oxford University’s Nuffield College.

I do research on social stratification, social demography, health and wellbeing, and work and family reconciliation. In my work, I mostly use quantitative methods and survey data. I have a PhD from the ICS/University of Groningen.

Elsewhere: a collection of Stata commands I have found useful at some point, and a collection of book reviews I wrote.
